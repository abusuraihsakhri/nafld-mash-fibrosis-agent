Interpretation limitations

    Research and educational support only. Scores are not a diagnosis, a substitute for clinical assessment or a method for determining histological fibrosis stage, MASH activity, or resmetirom eligibility. Do not use during acute illness.

    Methodology

    FIB-4 = age × AST / (platelets × √ALT). Low-risk threshold is 1.3 (2.0 if age >65 years); scores >2.67 are high risk. Intermediate or high results generally require secondary assessment with VCTE/ELF or specialist evaluation according to clinical context. FIB-4 has limited accuracy for those aged <35 years.

    NFS = −1.675 + 0.037×age + 0.094×BMI + 1.13×IFG/diabetes + 0.99×(AST/ALT) − 0.013×platelets − 0.66×albumin. NFS is provided for research comparability; it is not the preferred initial test in current stepwise MASLD guidance.

    Sources: AASLD 2023 Practice Guidance; EASL–EASD–EASO 2024 MASLD Guidelines; Angulo et al., 2007 (NFS).

    This browser calculator does not compute FAST, CAP-based steatosis, VCTE fibrosis staging, or drug eligibility. Laboratory values and calculations stay in the open page and are cleared on reset/refresh; no analytics or external script dependencies are used.
